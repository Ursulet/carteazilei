# PROMPT 11 — Analytics, Trust Pages & Affiliation

Use PROMPT 00.

## Objective

Instrument business outcomes and make trust visible.

## Events

Implement typed event contract for:
- recommendation_quiz_started
- recommendation_quiz_completed
- recommendation_result_shown
- recommendation_alternative_requested
- book_viewed
- daily_feature_viewed
- retailer_click
- recommendation_feedback_positive/negative
- book_started
- book_finished

Persist critical product events server-side where appropriate.

## Trust pages

Implement:
- `/despre`
- `/cum-recomandam`
- `/politica-editoriala`
- `/afiliere`
- `/echipa`
- `/editor/[slug]`
- `/contact`

## Methodology

Explain:
- editorial vs algorithmic recommendation;
- how books are tagged;
- how feedback helps;
- sponsored firewall.

## Affiliation

Near commercial CTA:
`Unele linkuri pot fi de afiliere. Dacă cumperi prin ele, Cartea Zilei poate primi un comision, fără cost suplimentar pentru tine. Acest lucru nu influențează recomandarea editorială.`

Adjust wording with legal review before launch.

## No fake trust

Do not create:
- press logos;
- reader counts;
- ratings;
- testimonials;
- awards;
unless backed by data supplied by owner.

## Dashboard

Add minimum internal metrics:
- quiz start/completion;
- result distribution;
- alternative rate;
- retailer CTR;
- feedback positive rate.
