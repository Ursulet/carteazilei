# PROMPT 16 — Launch Content & First 30 Days Operationalization

Use PROMPT 00.

This is not primarily a coding prompt. Act as technical/editorial implementation assistant.

## Objective

Prepare the product so the launch feels like an authority, not an empty platform.

## Build operational reports/admin filters for

- published books missing caveat;
- incomplete traits;
- missing editor;
- no similar relations;
- no retailer offer;
- stale offer;
- daily feature calendar gaps;
- SEO hubs below quality gate;
- book pages needing review.

## Launch content target

Owner/editorial team should aim for:
- 100 strong book pages;
- 20 author pages;
- 20+ useful hubs/lists;
- 10+ next-read pages;
- daily feature schedule for coming weeks;
- trust/methodology pages.

Do not fabricate content to hit counts.

## First 30 days dashboard

Expose:
- organic landings;
- indexation sample;
- quiz completion;
- recommendation positive feedback;
- alternative rate;
- retailer click rate;
- top zero/low-confidence situations;
- top pages by engagement.

## Optimization backlog

Generate a prioritized backlog only from real production data.

No major feature expansion before the core recommendation loop is validated.

STOP.
